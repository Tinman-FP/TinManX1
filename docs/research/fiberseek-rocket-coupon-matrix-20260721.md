# FibreSeek Rocket Coupon Matrix - 2026-07-21

This note records clean-room Rocket Slicer 1.3.1.480 observations from seven
neutral Z0 coupon models. The coupons were generated by TinManX1 tooling and
sent through Rocket's local `/api/slice/generate` endpoint with the bundled
FibreSeeker 3 PETG + X-CCF baseline.

The important model-prep lesson is that Rocket rejects simple probe models
centered around Z=0 as "No models to slice or models are too small to be
sliced." The corrected probe set sits on the build plate with minimum Z at 0.

## Conclusions

- The shipped PETG/X-CCF `Speedy` profile behaves as plastic-only on every
  corrected coupon, even though CFC materials and slot assignments are present
  in the request/session.
- `ReinForced` emits `Plastic and Composite`: plastic roads remain, but CFC
  routes appear on selected Z levels and use native `M1001` blocks.
- `Fortified` emits `Composite Only`: CFC route coverage increases by roughly
  an order of magnitude over medium on these coupons.
- Rocket's CFC blocks are overwhelmingly open paths. Closed-loop-heavy output
  remains an overlay signature and should fail TinManX1 parity review.
- Every fiber-bearing coupon in this matrix emitted `;CUT DISTANCE 54.8`.
- Heavy mode may still skip Z levels on thin/simple coupons when top/bottom
  shell or geometry constraints consume those layers; it should not be modeled
  as "every numeric layer always has fiber."

## TinManX1 Requirements Added By This Matrix

- Light must be a real behavior family, not a scalar copy of medium/heavy.
  For the shipped Rocket PETG/X-CCF baseline, light parity means plastic-only
  unless TinManX1 intentionally offers a separate user-selected "light fiber"
  strategy.
- Medium and heavy need separate manufacturing modes: medium parity is
  `Plastic and Composite`; heavy parity is `Composite Only`.
- The route selector must prefer legal long open routes and return rejected
  candidates as plastic fallback.
- Coupon fixtures used for Rocket automation must be normalized onto the build
  plate before submitting to Rocket.
